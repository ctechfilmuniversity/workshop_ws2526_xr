##  About the Project
Potion Panic is a Unity-based virtual reality project that combines exploration, narrative-driven gameplay, and puzzlebased challenges. 
The player explores a stylized 3D environment belonging to a sinister wizard, guided by a crow that provides indirect instructions and narrative cues.
Through environmental interaction and problem-solving, the player gathers ingredients required to brew a potion in order to escape the wizard and his hut. Interaction is central to the experience and includes a variety of mechanics such as grabbing, collecting, and changing the player’s scale, allowing the space to be explored in different ways. Most objects within the hut can be interacted with, encouraging an explorative approach to discovering the environment.

The game concludes with a subtle narrative twist, revealing that the escape is not what it initially seems. 
The project focuses on atmospheric world-building and magical elements, using a reduced visual aesthetic while balancing exploration with puzzle-based challenges.

## Used Software
- Unity with UnityXR
- Meta Quest 3 and 2
- Blender (Remodelling and Texturing)
- Photoshop (Custom Textures)

## Performance
While there is still room for more performance tweaking the game runs native on the Meta Quest 3 at stable 73 frames per second.
