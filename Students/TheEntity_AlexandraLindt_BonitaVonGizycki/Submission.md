# The Entity

*A VR experience where the player explores a surreal landscape,
follows subtle clues, and encounters a mysterious entity.*

## About the Project

This project is a narrative-driven VR experience focused on exploration,
atmosphere, and subtle guidance through objectives and sound.

The player explores a surreal landscape and follows environmental cues
such as sound, movement, and light. Throughout the experience, a mysterious
interdimensional entity attempts to communicate with the player.

Progression is achieved by finding and assembling fragmented puzzle shards.
The experience concludes once all fragments are reunited and the final
connection with the entity is established.
